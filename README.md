# Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of insurance policies and claim patterns. The objective of this project is to analyze policy distribution, understand customer demographics, and identify key factors that influence insurance claims.

By transforming raw insurance data into an interactive dashboard, this project helps uncover insights related to policy trends, risk factors, and claim behavior. The dashboard enables stakeholders to make informed decisions based on data-driven insights.

## Tools & Technologies Used

* Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard development
* Excel / CSV Dataset – Data source
* Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
* Data Modeling – Relationship building and calculated measures

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights several key insurance metrics:

* Total Policies: 37,542
* Total Claim Amount: $187.8M
* Average Claim Frequency: 0.5
* Average Claim Amount: $5K

These KPIs provide a quick overview of policy volume and claim performance across the portfolio.

## Dashboard Features

### Policy Distribution Analysis

The dashboard examines how insurance policies are distributed across different categories including:

* Vehicle Usage (Private vs Commercial)
* Car Manufacturer
* Coverage Zone (Urban, Suburban, Rural)

This helps identify which vehicle types and regions contribute the most to the policy portfolio.

### Customer Demographic Analysis

Customer demographic factors are analyzed to understand the characteristics of policyholders:

* Gender distribution
* Age group analysis
* Education level

Understanding demographic patterns helps insurers identify key customer segments.

### Claims Analysis

The dashboard evaluates claim trends across different segments including:

* Claim amount by education level
* Claim amount by marital status
* Average claim frequency

These insights help identify segments with higher claim exposure.

### Vehicle Analysis

Vehicle-related insights include:

* Total policies by vehicle manufacturing year
* Policies by car brand
* Policies by vehicle usage

These variables help insurers understand how vehicle characteristics influence policy distribution.

## Business Insights

### Policy Distribution

Private vehicle usage dominates the insurance portfolio, indicating that individual vehicle owners form the largest customer segment. Commercial vehicle policies represent a smaller portion but may carry higher operational risk.

### Customer Demographics

The highest number of policies comes from customers aged 26–55, suggesting that middle-aged individuals represent the primary insurance market. Gender distribution is relatively balanced, indicating equal policy participation among male and female customers.

### Claims Behavior

Claim amounts vary across demographic groups such as education level and marital status. Married customers appear to generate higher total claim amounts, possibly due to greater vehicle usage within households.

### Geographic Coverage

Policy distribution across urban, suburban, and rural zones appears balanced. This indicates a geographically diversified insurance portfolio, which can reduce concentration risk.

### Vehicle Trends

Newer vehicle models show higher policy counts, suggesting that customers prefer insuring relatively modern vehicles with higher market value.

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, insurance companies could:

* Focus marketing efforts on middle-aged customers who represent the largest policyholder group.
* Develop personalized insurance plans for different demographic segments.
* Monitor claim trends to improve underwriting and risk assessment.
* Implement risk-based pricing models based on vehicle characteristics and customer profiles.
* Use geographic data to optimize regional insurance offerings.

## Conclusion

The Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard demonstrates how data analytics can transform raw insurance data into meaningful insights. By analyzing policy distribution, customer demographics, vehicle characteristics, and claim trends, the dashboard helps identify patterns that influence insurance risk.

The analysis highlights that private vehicle owners in middle-age groups represent the core customer base, while demographic and vehicle-related factors influence claim patterns. These insights can support better pricing strategies, improved risk management, and more effective insurance product design.
